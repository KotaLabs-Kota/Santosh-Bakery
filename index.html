Our story

From one oven in Gumanpura
to four corners of Kota.

Santosh Bakers began in 1985 with a simple promise: bake it fresh, bake it eggless, and never let a family celebration be ordinary. Four decades on, the same recipes, the same ovens and the same family are still behind every cake that leaves our kitchen.

Today you'll find us in Gumanpura, R K Puram, Dadabari and Kunhadi — independent counters run by people who know their regulars by name. No franchise, no shortcuts, no frozen cakes.

Ask on WhatsApp
Are all your cakes eggless?
Yes — every cake, pastry and bake at all four Santosh Bakers outlets is 100% eggless. Our cakes are finished with fresh cream, so please refrigerate them and consume within 24–48 hours.
How much notice do you need for a custom cake?
Ready flavours are usually available the same day. For photo cakes, fondant work or themed designs, give us at least 24–48 hours. Wedding and bulk orders need 3–4 days.
Do you deliver?
You can pick up from any outlet, and local delivery is available around each outlet through our own team and on Swiggy & Zomato. Call your nearest counter with the delivery pin code to confirm timing and charges.
What is the smallest cake I can order?
Half‑kilo cakes are our smallest size — perfect for 4–6 people. Cakes are priced per kilo, so a 500 g cake is roughly half the listed price plus decoration.
Can I write a message on the cake?
Of course. Add your name or message in the “Plan your cake” form, or mention it on WhatsApp. Keep it short — 25–30 characters fits best on a 1 kg cake.
Are you on Swiggy and Zomato?
Yes — order from Swiggy or Zomato (Kothari Road / Chawani listing). Prices and offers there can differ from counter prices, so ordering directly on WhatsApp or at the outlet usually works out cheaper.
